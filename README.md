# Cosmic Weaponry, Drawn · อาวุธเทพ วาดด้วยคณิต

Weapons of the gods, drawn live with math, in English and Thai.

https://nanobotco.github.io/cosmic-weaponry/ · https://nanobotco.github.io/cosmic-weaponry/th/

- `tools/copy_text.py` holds all copy (EN + TH); `tools/build.py` writes `docs/index.html`, `docs/th/index.html`, `llms.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt` and `icon.svg`.
- `docs/app.js` draws everything: the 3-D vajra (one prong curve copied around the shaft), the bell's inharmonic partials (WebAudio), the dielectric breakdown model of lightning (Niemeyer, Pietronero & Wiesmann 1984) with a box-count dimension, Mekhala and Ramasun's light-then-sound clock, Widmanstätten bands from the faces of an octahedron, Vishvakarma's sun lathe, Brokkr's bellows and the fly, and the Monkey King's staff at fixed mass.
- `?card` at 1200×630 renders the share card (`docs/card.jpg`); `?t=` fixes the hero's moment.
- Photographs come from Wikimedia Commons (`tools/credits.json`), each credited on the page.
